Crank & Playback Configuration

Speed smoothing (attack)0.025

How gradually the measured crank speed feeds into playback tempo. Lower = smoother (sluggish). Higher = snappier (may sound choppy). Default: 0.025

Stop decay (release)1.5

How fast the speed drops to zero when you stop cranking. Higher = abrupt stop. Values > 1 give near-instant cutoff. Default: 1.5

Decay delay (50 ms windows)2

How many 50 ms windows of crank silence are tolerated before fast stop-decay starts. Higher values help absorb brief mid-crank pauses. Default: 2 (100 ms)

Pause threshold (rev/s)0.25

Crank speed below which playback pauses. Must be lower than the resume threshold. Default: 0.25 rev/s

Resume threshold (rev/s)0.70

Crank speed above which paused playback resumes. A wider gap between pause and resume thresholds reduces false triggers. Default: 0.70 rev/s

Volume fade speed1

Volume units stepped per 10 ms tick during fade-in/out when cranking starts or stops. 1 = slow fade (~700 ms at full volume). 10 = fast fade (~70 ms). Default: 1

Dimmer fade-in (stop→start) ms1000

Global dimmer fade-in duration after crank restarts. Range: 0–5000 ms. Default: 1000 ms.

Dimmer fade-out (start→stop) ms1000

Global dimmer fade-out duration when crank stops. Range: 0–5000 ms. Default: 1000 ms.

Crank direction

Filter out reverse cranking. “Direction A” and “Direction B” correspond to the two physical turn directions — try both to find which matches your normal cranking direction. Default: Direction B only

Light Organ (FFT)

Bass weight45

Multiplier applied to the square-root of the bass energy (80–250 Hz). Higher values make the lamp react more strongly to bass. Applied as: level = sqrtf(bass) × bass_weight + sqrtf(mid) × mid_weight. Default: 45

Mid weight5.0

Multiplier applied to the square-root of the mid-range energy (250–2000 Hz). Raise this to add attack/snare contribution; lower it to keep the lamp purely bass-driven. Default: 5

Range decay rate0.998

Per-frame (50 ms) decay of the auto-range peak. Lower values make the auto-range adapt faster (more sensitive at low volume), higher values keep the full range for longer (smoother but slower adaptation). Range 0.990–0.999. Default: 0.998

Sync offset (ms)0

Timing offset between the audio file position and the FFT analysis window. Positive = lamp flashes before the beat (use for incandescent lamps with thermal lag ~100–200 ms); negative = lamp flashes after. Tune until lamp visually matches the beat. Range −1000–+1000 ms. Default: 0
